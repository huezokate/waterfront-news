## Context

Research three streams before any design: (1) mobile UX & engagement for location-based
audio/walking tour apps, geolocation media layering, progressive disclosure for
hierarchical historical content; (2) translating a Victorian-newspaper aesthetic to a
usable mobile UI + "living image" motion techniques; (3) WCAG 2.2 AA for a textured,
serif, motion-heavy print aesthetic, including a contrast-verified palette.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A consolidated research deliverable covering all three streams with named real-world
  examples and source URLs.
- A concrete, contrast-computed Victorian palette usable as design tokens.
- Top actionable recommendations distilled for TimeLens.
